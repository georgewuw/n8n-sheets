n8n-sheets

n8n-sheets is a personal, single-user automation application built and operated by its owner for private use only. It is not a public service and offers no sign-up.

What the app does

The app connects the owner's own Google Sheets and Google Drive accounts to a self-hosted n8n workflow instance running on the owner's private server. It is used to manage a personal content-production queue:

  • Topics for social-media posts are kept in a Google Sheets spreadsheet (the "topic queue").
  • A scheduled workflow reads the queued topics, drafts an article with an image for each topic, and packages the result as a ZIP file.
  • The ZIP files are uploaded to a designated folder in the owner's Google Drive, and the workflow writes the status and the file link back into the spreadsheet.

Who uses it

Only the owner. The app signs in with the owner's own Google account through Google OAuth and acts solely on the owner's own spreadsheets and files.
